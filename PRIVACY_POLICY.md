# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 March 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Taxi Rider and Taxi Driver Android apps operated by The Five Products Group ("we", "our", "us"). The two apps use a shared Firebase backend. This Policy explains what information these apps process, how that information is used, and when it is shared.

These apps currently support map-based rider/driver discovery, ride hails, rider-to-rider messaging, optional phone-number sign-in, a premium subscription, and competition participation. They do not ask you to create an email/password account, do not currently provide rider-driver chat, and do not collect in-app card or bank payment details for rides. Subscription payments are handled by Google Play rather than directly by us.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the apps, we may process the following information:

- **Account and profile information**: a Firebase account identifier, your phone number if you choose phone verification, your chosen profile name, general status text, selected avatar, optional uploaded profile photo, premium/subscriber status, and an optional sign-up code entered in the app.
- **Location and map presence data**: your precise device location while the app is open and location permission is enabled, plus related map-presence fields such as geohash, last updated time, driver availability, driver route/destination text, ride-hail state, and pickup intent information.
- **Messaging and safety data**: rider-to-rider direct messages, message timestamps, contact/thread summaries, unread/read state stored on your device, and rider/driver block lists.
- **Competition data**: competition score/activity tallies, whether you agreed to the latest competition terms, the agreed version/hash/time, and related competition status data retrieved from Firebase Remote Config.
- **Subscription, ads, and consent data**: Google Play subscription entitlement status, ad-consent responses, advertising-related identifiers and ad request data used by Google Mobile Ads for non-premium users.
- **Device and service data**: app, device, network, diagnostic, analytics, and integrity-check data processed by Google/Firebase SDKs used in the apps, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, and Firebase App Check / Play Integrity.
- **Local app storage**: app preferences and cached state stored on your device, such as keep-screen-on preference, the phone number you entered for verification, sign-up code state, competition-agreement cache, and last-read rider message markers.

If you choose a custom profile photo, the image is cropped in the app and then uploaded for use as your profile picture.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

- create and maintain anonymous or phone-verified accounts;
- show nearby riders or drivers on the map and keep presence information current;
- let riders hail drivers and let drivers accept or decline those hails;
- provide rider-to-rider messaging and block/unblock controls;
- apply premium subscription benefits, including removing ads for subscribers;
- request ad consent and show ads to non-premium users;
- run competition features, tally eligible activity, and record rules acceptance;
- protect the service through authentication, security rules, fraud/abuse checks, and App Check / device integrity checks;
- support account migration when an anonymous account is linked to a phone-verified account; and
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the apps.

---

## 3. Sharing of Information

We may share information in the following ways:

- **With other app users**:
  - Riders can see nearby drivers' live map location, route/destination text, availability/status, profile name/status, avatar, optional profile photo, and premium marker state.
  - Drivers can see nearby riders' live map location, profile name/status, avatar, optional profile photo, and hail/request state needed to operate the service.
  - If a rider enables rider-to-rider messaging mode, that rider's public messaging profile and live location are made visible to signed-in users so nearby rider messaging can work.
  - Rider-to-rider messages are stored in each participant's message thread and are intended to be visible only to the participants in that conversation.
- **With service providers and platform providers**: including Google/Firebase services used by the apps such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, Analytics, App Check / Play Integrity, Google Maps, Google Mobile Ads / UMP, and Google Play Billing.
- **For legal or safety reasons**: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect users, the apps, or our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 4. Data Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to operate the apps, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms.

More specifically:

- account, profile, map-presence, hail, block-list, and subscription-status data may remain in Firebase while your app profile is active;
- rider-to-rider message history is designed to retain recent messages only, with older messages and stale message threads pruned by the app after about 30 days;
- competition records may be retained longer where needed for verification, fraud prevention, or legal record-keeping; and
- some local app data stored on your device may remain until you clear app storage, uninstall the app, or overwrite that data through normal use.

---

## 5. Data Deletion and Your Choices

Both apps include in-app deletion controls:

- **Delete Data** removes the app's Firebase profile data and related stored records used by that app.
- **Delete Profile** removes the app data above and also requests deletion of the Firebase Authentication profile for that Firebase account. Because the Rider and Driver apps share a backend, deleting that Firebase account may affect access across both apps if you use the same account in both.

For Rider, deleting app data is designed to remove the rider profile document, rider public messaging document, rider contact/message threads, and rider block list. For Driver, deleting app data is designed to remove the driver profile document and driver block list.

Some local device data may remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Some provider logs, backups, or legally required records may also remain for a limited period.

If you need help with deletion or have a privacy request, contact us at **fiveproductsgroup@gmail.com**.

## 6. Data Security

We use provider security features such as authenticated access, Firebase security rules, App Check, and related Google/Firebase protections to reduce unauthorized access. No system is completely secure, and use of the apps is at your own risk.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information through the apps, contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.
